OUR PURPOSE

Rooted in nature.
Driven by community.

Our mission, vision and specific objectives guide the work of Africa Push Ministry Ntambutsa.

OUR MISSION

Protecting nature, empowering communities.

To develop activities throughout the national territory of Burundi that safeguard the environment, promote the sustainable management of land, and involve communities in the protection of nature.

OUR VISION

A society living in harmony with nature.

We want a society in which every member of the community lives in harmony with their natural environment.

OUR SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES

01

Safeguard and protect the environment.

02

Connect communities with nature.

03

Promote and support public education and introduce people to environmental professions and the discovery of nature.

04

Contribute to food self-sufficiency by promoting conservation agriculture.
