About Africa Push Ministry.

Africa Push Ministry (APM) Ntambutsa works to protect nature, promote sustainable land management and involve communities in environmental protection throughout Burundi.

Who we are

Nous sommes une organisation communautaire pour la préservation de l'environnement.

We are a community-based organisation dedicated to environmental preservation.

OUR PURPOSE

Our mission and vision.

These statements express the purpose and future that guide Africa Push Ministry Ntambutsa.

OUR MISSION

Protecting the environment with communities.

To carry out activities throughout the national territory of Burundi to safeguard the environment, promote sustainable land management and involve communities in the protection of nature.

OUR VISION

A society living in harmony with nature.

We want a society in which every member of the community lives in harmony with their natural environment.

OUR SPECIFIC OBJECTIVES

01

Safeguard and protect the environment.

02

Connect communities with nature.

03

Promote and support public education, and introduce the population to environmental activities and the discovery of nature.

04

Contribute to food self-sufficiency by promoting conservation agriculture.

Our commitment

We encourage practical environmental action, community involvement and sustainable approaches to the management of land and natural resources.

Work with us

We welcome dialogue with communities, individuals and organisations interested in nature protection, environmental education and conservation agriculture.
