About APM Ntambutsa.

African Push Ministry Ntambutsa is a space for people to connect, exchange knowledge and explore opportunities to contribute to their communities.

Who we are

APM Ntambutsa aims to encourage a culture of collaboration, learning and shared initiative. We value the experience, ideas and skills that each person can bring to a collective effort.

Our mission

To create connections and encourage learning and cooperation that help people develop their abilities and take part in meaningful community initiatives.

Our vision

A connected community where people can share knowledge, develop their potential and work together towards positive change.

Our values

Respect, integrity, inclusion, responsibility, cooperation and a commitment to continuous learning.

Our approach

Listen to people, share ideas, build partnerships and support practical action.
